What is profit margin?

Profit margin shows how much profit you make from each sale after subtracting the product cost. It helps ecommerce store owners understand if a product is profitable or not.

Formula

Margin % = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Selling price × 100

Example

If a product sells for $50 and costs $20, the profit is $30 and the margin is 60%.

FAQ

What is a good ecommerce profit margin?

A typical ecommerce profit margin ranges between 20% and 40%, depending on the product and advertising costs.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Profit margin is calculated using the formula: (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Selling price × 100.

Why is profit margin important?

Profit margin helps ecommerce store owners determine whether a product is profitable after costs such as product price, shipping and advertising.
