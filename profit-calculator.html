Ecommerce Profit Calculator

What is ecommerce profit?

Ecommerce profit is the money you keep after subtracting all costs related to selling a product online. These costs usually include product cost, advertising, shipping and payment processing fees.

Formula

Profit = Selling price − Product cost − Shipping cost − Advertising cost − Payment fee

Example

If a product sells for $50, costs $20, shipping costs $5 and advertising costs $10, the profit would be $15.

FAQ

How do you calculate ecommerce profit?

Ecommerce profit is calculated by subtracting all business costs from the selling price of the product. These costs include product cost, shipping, marketing and payment fees.

Why is profit calculation important?

Profit calculation helps store owners understand whether a product is profitable or not before scaling advertising campaigns.

What is a good ecommerce profit?

Many ecommerce businesses aim for a profit of at least 20–30% after all costs are included.
