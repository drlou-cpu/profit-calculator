Ecommerce Profit Calculator

What is ecommerce profit?

Ecommerce profit is the amount of money you keep after subtracting all costs such as product cost, shipping and ads.

Formula

Profit = Selling price − Cost − Shipping − Ads − Fees

Example

If you sell at $50 and your total costs are $35, your profit is $15.

FAQ

Is profit important?

Yes, it tells you if your product is actually making money.

What is a good profit?

Most ecommerce stores aim for 20% to 40% profit.
