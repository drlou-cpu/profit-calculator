What is ecommerce profit?

Ecommerce profit is the money you keep after subtracting all costs related to selling a product online.

Formula

Profit = Selling price – Cost – Shipping – Ads – Fees

Example

If you sell for $50 and costs are $35 → profit = $15

FAQ

How do you calculate profit?
Subtract all costs from selling price.

Why is profit important?
It tells if your business is profitable.

Good margin?
20%–40% is common.
