What is ecommerce profit?

Ecommerce profit is the money you keep after subtracting all costs related to selling a product online. These include product cost, advertising, shipping, and payment fees.

Formula

Profit = Selling price − Cost − Shipping − Ads − Fees

Example

If you sell a product for $50 and your total costs are $35, your profit is $15.

FAQ

How do you calculate ecommerce profit?
Subtract all costs from the selling price including ads and fees.

Why is profit important?
It helps you know if your business is actually making money.

What is a good profit margin?
Most ecommerce businesses aim for 20% to 40% profit.
