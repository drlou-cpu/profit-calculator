Ecommerce ROAS Calculator

What is ROAS?

ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) measures how much revenue you generate for every dollar spent on advertising. It is one of the most important metrics for ecommerce businesses running paid ads.

Formula

ROAS = Revenue from ads ÷ Ad spend

Example

If you spend $100 on ads and generate $400 in revenue, your ROAS is 4. This means you earn $4 for every $1 spent on ads.

FAQ

What is a good ROAS?

A good ROAS depends on your profit margins, but many ecommerce businesses aim for a ROAS of 3 or higher.

Why is ROAS important?

ROAS helps businesses understand whether their advertising campaigns are profitable or not.

How do you improve ROAS?

You can improve ROAS by increasing conversion rates, reducing advertising costs, and optimizing product pricing and marketing strategies.
