Ecommerce Markup Calculator

What is markup?

Markup is the percentage increase from the product cost to the selling price. It shows how much you increase the price compared to the cost.

Formula

Markup % = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Example

If a product costs $20 and you sell it for $50, the markup is 150%.

FAQ

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is based on cost, while margin is based on selling price. They are not the same.

What is a good markup?

A common markup in ecommerce ranges between 50% and 200% depending on the product and competition.
